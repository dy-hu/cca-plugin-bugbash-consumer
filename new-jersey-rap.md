# New Jersey Rap

From the Turnpike to the Shore, let me tell you what's in store,
Garden State, represent, we got so much to explore.
Newark to Cape May, Atlantic City lights,
Jersey nights alive with energy, burning so bright.

Pork roll, egg, and cheese on a hard roll every day,
Diners open 24/7, always got something to say.
Bruce Springsteen on the speakers, pumping through the night,
Bon Jovi's "Living on a Prayer," feeling alright.

Exit numbers on the Parkway, we know every single one,
The Meadowlands at sunset, the grind is never done.
Hoboken skyline glowing, Manhattan in our view,
Jersey City rising up, the comeback's long overdue.

Sandy beaches at Asbury, Seaside, Long Branch too,
Wildwood boardwalks calling, the sky an endless blue.
Jughandles keep us turning, our traffic's on a grid,
Pumping gas, the only state where that's still what we did.

So don't sleep on New Jersey, we're stronger than the hate,
The most densely populated, but undeniably great.
From the Pine Barrens to the Highlands, from the north down to the south,
Jersey pride runs deep, and we're not afraid to shout it out.
